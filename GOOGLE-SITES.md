# Make BATCAVE work at school

School filters usually allow Google Sites. They often block game hosts.

## Already live

Open: https://fungamer1234.github.io/batcave/

Leave **School** turned on. Games marked SCHOOL open on classroom6x Google Site pages.

## If github.io is blocked at school

1. Sign into Google (school account is fine).
2. Go to https://sites.google.com and click **Blank site**.
3. Name it **BATCAVE**.
4. Insert the Batman images from this folder (emblem, cave, batman).
5. Insert > **Embed** > **By URL** > `https://fungamer1234.github.io/batcave/`
6. If that embed is blank at school, skip it and add buttons/links to:
   `https://sites.google.com/view/classroom6x/`
7. Publish. Web address can be `batcave`.

Your school URL will look like `https://sites.google.com/view/batcave`.
